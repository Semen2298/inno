Our way of working

A small studio. A shared curiosity.

We are drawn to homes that feel easy to live in. Not a picture-perfect room that nobody uses, but a place with a well-worn table, good light and enough space to be yourself. That is where our conversations begin.

We listen closely, then make the choices clear.

We favour useful spaces and materials with a long life.

A room should make you want to stay a little longer. That is a good place to start.

From conversation to construction

A clear path, with room to think.

01

Listen

We talk about your routines, your site and your priorities. Together, we write a brief and agree what the project needs to do.

A clear brief and shared priorities.

02

Explore

Plans, sketches and a simple model let us test ideas together. We talk through the trade-offs before choosing a direction.

A concept you can understand and discuss.

03

Resolve

We bring materials, lighting and technical details into one coordinated design, reviewing the decisions that matter most.

Drawings and a considered material palette.

04

Make

The design becomes a guide for the people making it. Questions are worked through carefully as the spaces take shape.

A shared reference for bringing the design to life.

A good place to begin
