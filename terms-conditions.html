Terms of use

INNO is a fictional studio presented as a frontend portfolio project. This website demonstrates layout, imagery, content and browser interactions.

Illustrative content

The homes, studio scenes and team portraits are generated illustrations. Names, roles, services and contact details are demonstration content, not evidence of a trading business, completed buildings or professional appointments.

Trying the features

The house study changes between illustrative views. The enquiry form validates sample entries locally and opens a confirmation page. It does not deliver a message, reserve a consultation, accept payments or create a contract.

Materials and credits

Fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Interface icons are from Lucide, with their license included in the project. Generated project imagery was prepared specifically for this portfolio.

External services

Links to maps, telephone and email applications lead outside this demonstration. No response or service is promised through the illustrative contact information.
